Bio

I'm a game designer and programmer from Abu Dhabi, currently studying Games Programming at SAE University College Dubai.

I've loved games for as long as I can remember. As a kid I was always coming up with ideas for my own, but I didn't have the skills to build any of them. Learning to design and program games is how I'm finally turning those ideas into things people can play.

My path here wasn't a straight line. After my A Levels, I spent a year at York University in Toronto, but I realised it wasn't what I wanted to do for the rest of my life. So I came back home and chose the field that suits me best.

I'm most interested in designing systems that give players meaningful choices. I like being able to program my own ideas, so I can prototype, playtest and improve them myself instead of leaving them on paper.

As a player, League of Legends is my go-to, along with Apex Legends. I also love story-driven games like God of War, Assassin's Creed IV: Black Flag and Elden Ring, and roguelikes like The Binding of Isaac, Enter the Gungeon, Hades and Balatro. Roguelikes are a big influence on how I think about replayability and building systems that combine in interesting ways.
